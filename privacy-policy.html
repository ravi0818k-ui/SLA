Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

In short: we collect only what we need to run your classes, we never sell it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

Super Learner Academy (“we”, “us”) runs the website www.superlearneracademy.in and teaches live online classes on learning skills. It is run by Abhishek Ranjan and operates online from India. For anything about your data, email support@superlearneracademy.in.

What we collect and why

When you register for a class

Your name, WhatsApp number and (if you give it) email address, plus whether you are a student or a parent and the class, entered on our registration form or sent to us on WhatsApp. We use them to send you the class details and joining link, to remind you about sessions and to answer your questions. Free registrations are saved in a Google Sheet in our own Google account, which only our team can open. If you are under 18, please register with your parent’s permission. For a paid workshop, the payment itself is handled by our payment partner; we never see or store your card or UPI details.

In our WhatsApp group and live classes

Class updates are shared in a WhatsApp group, where other members can see your name and phone number, as in any WhatsApp group. Classes run on Zoom, where other participants can see the display name you choose. WhatsApp’s and Zoom’s own privacy policies apply to those apps.

When you email or message us

We keep your message and our reply so we can help you and follow up.

When you visit the website

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads tags. They use cookies and similar technology to tell us how many people visit, which pages they read, the type of device, the approximate location (city level) and which ad or link brought them here. This helps us improve the site and see whether our ads work. It does not tell us who you are.

What stays only in your browser

Your quiz answers, language choice, mind maps, notes you turn into a PDF, and small settings (for example, a closed banner) are saved in your own browser only. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with the services we need to run the classes: our registration and payment partner, WhatsApp, Zoom and Google (Google Sheets for the registration list, analytics and ads measurement). We may also share it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your registration details while you take part in our classes and for as long as we need them to answer questions about your registration. Payment records are kept for as long as Indian law requires. After that, we delete them. You can ask us to delete your details earlier (see below).

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or stop using it (withdraw your consent);
  • look into a complaint about how we handled it.

Email support@superlearneracademy.in from the email address or with the phone number you registered with. We will reply as soon as we can.

Grievance contact: Abhishek Ranjan, support@superlearneracademy.in

Students under 18

Many of our students are school students. If you are under 18, please register with your parent’s or guardian’s permission. A parent or guardian can email us at any time to see or delete their child’s details.

Cookies: your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also turn off Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and control personalised Google ads in My Ad Center. The website works without these cookies.

Keeping it safe

Only the people who run the classes can see your registration details, and the website uses a secure (https) connection. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care, and we will tell you if a problem affects your data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date below.
