About Us

Who Runs Super Learner Academy

One coach, one subject: teaching students how to learn — so the hours they already put in actually show up in their marks.

What this site is

Super Learner Academy is an independent online training program for students, run by Abhishek Ranjan. We teach practical, science-informed learning skills — memory techniques, focus and concentration, active recall and spaced revision, and the growth mindset that keeps students using them.

Everything published here is aimed at one specific problem: a student studies hard, re-reads and highlights for hours, and still forgets it by exam day. We don't teach the syllabus. We teach the method students were never taught — and the free tools on this site let anyone try a piece of that method before paying for anything.

The site sells one paid product: a 3-day live workshop on Zoom, taught live by Abhishek himself. Nothing else on this site is behind a paywall.

How we decide what to teach

Learning advice is full of claims that sound impressive and don't survive contact with evidence. We hold our content to a simple line: if we teach a technique, we explain the mechanism behind it.

What we teach

  • Active recall and spaced practice — the retrieval and spacing effects
  • Mnemonic systems (the Hook System, memory palaces) and how they use visual and spatial memory
  • Attention and concentration training, and how to build a revision schedule that survives a real timetable
  • Growth mindset and self-talk as the habits that decide whether a student keeps using the method

What we will not claim

  • “Photographic” or eidetic memory — we don't teach it, because it isn't a trainable skill
  • “You only use 10% of your brain” and similar neuro-myths
  • A guaranteed score or percentage increase — results depend on the student's subject, starting point and follow-through, and anyone promising you a number is guessing

About the testimonials

Student quotes on this site describe what those students experienced. We do not attach invented score figures to them. If you see a claim anywhere on this site that you think is wrong or overstated, email us — we will correct it.

Free tools on this site

Both are genuinely free, need no sign-up, and run entirely in your browser.

  • Know Yourself Better — 19 self-assessments (learning style, focus, study habits, memory, mindset) and 11 brain-training tools. Your answers stay in your own browser; we don't collect them.

Payments, refunds and privacy

Payments

Workshop payments are handled by our payment partner over UPI, cards and net banking. We never see or store your card or UPI credentials.

Refunds

If you attend the full workshop and feel it didn't add value, contact us within 24 hours of the final session for a complete refund. Email support@superlearneracademy.in with the name and email address you registered with.

Your data

We collect the registration details you enter when you pay (name, email, phone) so we can send you the joining link, and we use Google Analytics and Google Ads tags to measure how the site performs. The free quizzes and the notes generator store what you type in your own browser only — those answers and files are never uploaded to us. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email us.
