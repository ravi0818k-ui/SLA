LEARNING SCIENCE

Yeh kaam kyun karta hai?

Motivation ki baatein nahi. Har technique is par based hai ki dimaag yaad kaise rakhta hai.

Yaad ko “hook” chahiye

Nayi baat tab tikti hai jab woh kisi jaani-pehchaani cheez se jud jaaye. Hook System yahi sikhata hai.

Dobara padhne se behtar: khud se yaad karna

Page dekh kar sab familiar lagta hai, par yaad nahi hota. Khud se answer nikalne se memory mazboot hoti hai.

Bhoolna ek pattern hai

Memory ek tay curve par fade hoti hai (Ebbinghaus ka forgetting curve). Sahi time par revision use reset karta hai.

Focus practice se badhta hai

Concentration janam se milne wala gun nahi. Drills se yeh muscle ki tarah train hota hai.

Dimaag badal sakta hai

Neuroplasticity: ability fixed nahi hai. Practice se dimaag naye connections banata hai.

Shaant dimaag zyada yaad karta hai

Exam ka stress recall mein rukawat daalta hai. Breathing aur meditation se dimaag clear rehta hai.
