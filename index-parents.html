KYA YEH AAPKE GHAR KI KAHANI HAI?

Har shaam ek hi sawaal: itni mehnat ke baad bhi marks kyun nahi?

“Tuition mein sab sahi, exam mein galat”

Ghar par tutor ke saamne har sawaal ka jawab. Paper mein wahi sawaal khaali chhod aaya.

“Kal raat yaad tha, aaj bhool gaya”

Raat ko poora chapter sunaya. Exam ke din aadha bhi yaad nahi aaya.

“PTM mein wahi baat: smart hai, par dhyaan nahi deta”

Har PTM mein yahi sunte hain. Par dhyaan lagana kisi ne sikhaya hi nahi.

“Aata tha, phir bhi silly mistakes”

Spelling, tables, plus-minus – sab aata tha, par jaldi mein galat likh diya.

“Paper dekhte hi ghabra jaata hai”

Ghar par chahakta hai. Exam hall mein chup aur nervous.

“Rishtedaar poochte hain: kitne marks aaye?”

Aur aap sochte hain: itna kharcha, itni mehnat – phir bhi kyun?

Aapka bachcha kamzor nahi hai. Bas use yaad rakhna kisi ne sikhaya nahi.

TUITION vs EXAM

Ghar par sab aata hai, exam mein nahi. Kyun?

Kyunki tuition aur exam do bilkul alag jagah hain. Farq dekhiye:

Tuition meinExam mein
Tutor paas baitha hai – atakte hi hint mil jaata haiKoi hint nahi – sab khud se yaad karna hai
Aaj padhaya, aaj hi poochaHafton baad poocha – beech mein bhool gaya
Wahi sawaal, wahi shabdSawaal ghuma kar, naye shabdon mein
Bol kar jawab, galti turant sudharLikh kar jawab, ghadi ki tik-tik
Ghar ka aaraamNaya mahaul aur exam ka darr

Tuition mein bachcha jawab pehchaanta hai; exam mein use khud se yaad karna padta hai. Yeh ek alag skill hai – aur yeh practice se seekhi ja sakti hai.

ASLI WAJAH

Galti bachche ki nahi – padhne ke tareeke ki hai

Zyada tuition ya zyada daant se yeh nahi badalta. Yeh badalta hai sahi method se – jo aap ghar par roz karwa sakte hain.

Pehchanna aur yaad karna alag hai

Tutor ya kitaab saamne ho toh sab jaana-pehchana lagta hai. Exam mein bina madad ke khud se yaad karna padta hai.

Ek baar padha, kuch din mein fade

Bhoolna dimaag ka natural pattern hai. Sahi gap par chhota revision na ho, toh exam tak bahut kuch nikal jaata hai.

Ratta sawaal badalte hi gayab

Bina samjhe yaad kiya jawab wahi shabd maangta hai. Kahani aur hooks se juda yaad tikta hai.

Darr yaad ko rok deta hai

Ghabrahat mein jo aata hai woh bhi bahar nahi aata. Shaant rehna bhi sikhaya ja sakta hai.

WORKSHOP KE BAAD, GHAR PAR

Aap bachche ke saath yeh 5 cheezein roz kar payenge

  • Bachche ko teacher banaiye: padhne ke baad woh aapko samjhaye (Feynman technique) – ratta nahi, samajh.
  • Band copy quiz: copy band karke 3 sawaal – khud se yaad karne ki roz ki aadat.
  • Thoda-thoda revision: test se ek raat pehle nahi, sahi gap par chhote revision.
  • Kahani aur hooks: tables, spellings aur lists yaad karne ke mazedaar tareeke.
  • Exam se pehle calm routine: chhoti breathing practice, taaki paper dekh kar ghabrahat na ho.

SAWAAL-JAWAB

Aapke sawaal

Mera bachcha Class 3 ya 4 mein hai (8–10 saal). Kya yeh uske liye hai?

Haan, aap dono ke liye. Workshop ki techniques – yaad karne ke hooks, khud se yaad karna, chhota revision aur exam se pehle shaant rehna – chhote bachchon ke saath bhi kaam aati hain. Class 3–5 ke liye parent bachche ke saath baith kar attend karein: aap method seekhiye, aur ghar par roz thodi der bachche ke saath practice karwaiye. Sessions sabke liye ek saath hote hain; chhote bachchon ka alag batch nahi hai.

Kya end mein koi aur course bechenge?

Nahi. Yahi poora course hai – teeno din ki saari techniques, worksheets, certificate aur WhatsApp community isi fees mein shamil hain. Session ke end mein koi sales pitch nahi hoti.

Bachcha pehle se tuition jaata hai. Kya yeh ek aur tuition hai?

Nahi. Workshop koi subject nahi padhata. Yeh sikhata hai ki jo school aur tuition mein padha, use yaad kaise rakhein aur exam mein khud se kaise likhein. Tuition chalti rahe – yeh use zyada kaam ka banata hai.

Kya mujhe (parent ko) bhi baithna hoga?

Chhote bachchon ke liye haan, yahi sabse accha hai. Aap method samjhenge toh ghar par roz karwa payenge – aur bachche ko aapka saath bhi milega. Ek hi seat par aap dono ek screen se join kar sakte hain.

Hinglish mein (Hindi + English), taaki parents aur bachche dono aasani se samajh sakein.

Class kab aur kaise hoti hai?

Kya isse marks sach mein badhenge?

Hum koi marks ya number promise nahi karte. Workshop aapko method deta hai; badlav tab aata hai jab aap ghar par use regularly karwate hain. Jo hum dete hain: dobara-dobara padhne ki jagah khud se yaad karna aur sahi gap par revision ka ek clear, aasaan system.

Kya yeh real science par based hai?

Haan. Active recall (khud se yaad karna) aur spaced practice (sahi gap par revision) learning research mein sabse effective study techniques maani jaati hain. Har technique ke saath aap yeh bhi samjhenge ki woh dimaag par kaam kyun karti hai.
